Common Test Abbreviations

CBC
Complete Blood Count - measures overall blood health.
HbA1c
Average blood glucose level over the past three months.
LFT
Liver Function Test - checks liver enzymes and health.
KFT
Kidney Function Test - checks kidney performance.
ECG
Electrocardiogram - records the electrical activity of the heart.
