Test Details - Complete Blood Count (CBC)

Test Name: Complete Blood Count (CBC)

Purpose: Used to evaluate overall health and detect a wide range of disorders such as anemia, infection, and blood cancers.


Sample Required: 2-3 ml of venous blood (EDTA vial)


Preparation: No special preparation required. Fasting is not necessary for this test.


Reference Range: Hemoglobin: 13-17 g/dL (Male), 12-15 g/dL (Female)


Report Time: Same day, within 4-6 hours

Important Instructions

  • Inform the technician about any current medication.
  • Avoid strenuous exercise before sample collection.
  • Fasting Requirement: Not required for CBC, but 8-12 hours fasting is required for Lipid Profile and Blood Sugar tests.

Note: Reference ranges may vary slightly between laboratories.
