About Laboratory Services

Our Clinical Laboratory offers a wide range of pathology and diagnostic services including blood tests, urine tests, thyroid profiling, liver and kidney function tests, lipid profiles, and diabetes screening. Our facility is equipped with modern automated analyzers operated by trained and experienced laboratory technicians, ensuring accurate results within the shortest possible turnaround time.

Pathalogy Tips

  • Stay hydrated before giving a blood sample.
  • Fast for 8-12 hours before a lipid profile test.
  • Carry your doctor's prescription.
  • Collect reports on time.
